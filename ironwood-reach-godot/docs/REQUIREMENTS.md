- `_test_visual_navigation_clearance`: actual generated mesh vertices for six building types fit their horizontal authority footprints; three soldier types, sampled over four animation states and 40 phases, fit the four-soldier battalion clearance; sampled worker geometry fits its separate body radius. This measures geometry, not rendered animation or every possible path.

The separate supplement below covers isolated Shield damage reduction and Overcharge boosts; Mending's meaningful cooperative tradeoff remains unestablished.

- `_test_far_building_attack_approach`: reproduces the browser-discovered home-to-enemy-keep approach; the full formation clears buildings, enters unchanged melee range, damages and destroys the full-health keep in an isolated scenario. A separate normally spawned raid damages the home keep. This is automated production-rule coverage, not a played battle or balance result.
- `_test_deterministic_replay`: one seed (`41`), identical commands and 360 fixed steps reproduce identical complete snapshots on this engine. This does not establish cross-platform determinism or all seeds/orderings.
